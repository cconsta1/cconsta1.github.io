I'm a theoretical nuclear physicist working where physics meets machine learning.

With Vaia Prassa (University of Thessaly), I compute potential energy surfaces for heavy and medium-heavy nuclei using covariant density functional theory, building the data for machine learning in nuclear theory. At the Université Libre de Bruxelles I built emulators of those surfaces that reached an **R squared of 0.98** on nuclei they had never seen.

I also build machine learning for osteoarchaeology and forensic science. That work is published in the *International Journal of Osteoarchaeology* and the *Journal of Forensic and Legal Medicine*, and I built [SexEst](https://sexest.cyi.ac.cy/) and [AgeEst](https://ageest.hpcf.cyi.ac.cy/).

My generative art has been exhibited at Art Week Volterra, won the La Grande Bellezza Award, sold to private collectors, ended up on a band t-shirt for [The Links](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC6Di34k3n_YXZ5t2xBAgakQ), and appeared as animations in a live show by [Sabina Odone](https://www.youtube.com/user/SabinaOdone) in Mexico City. I also run a physics [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@doctor_no_).
